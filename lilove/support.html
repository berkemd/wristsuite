Support / Destek

Write to brkekahraman@icloud.com. One person reads it; expect a reply within two working days. Tell us your account email and what you were doing when it went wrong — that is usually enough.

English

I can't sign in

LiLove needs an account: your habits and goals live on our server so they survive a lost phone. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use the same button you used the first time — an email/password account and an "Sign in with Apple" account are two different accounts even with the same address. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, sign in with Apple again rather than typing the relay address.

Restoring a subscription on a new device

Sign in, open Premium, and tap Restore Purchases. Your subscription follows your Apple Account, and we re-check it with Apple every time. If it still does not appear, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account that bought it (iPhone Settings → your name).

I bought coins and they did not arrive

Open the app again while online. Apple re-delivers a purchase that was never finished, and the app completes it on launch — coins land then. If they still do not, write to us with the date and the pack size; every purchase carries an App Store transaction identifier and we can look yours up. You will never be charged twice for the same transaction: the server refuses to credit a transaction identifier it has already seen.

Managing or cancelling a subscription

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LiLove. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com — we cannot issue them.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your profile, your habits, goals, tasks, notes and coach history from our database. It does not cancel an active App Store subscription — cancel that in iPhone Settings, otherwise it keeps renewing.

Does LiLove work offline?

No. Your content is stored on our server, so LiLove needs a connection to sign in, to load your day and to save what you change. We would rather say this plainly than have you find out on a plane.

What the coach can and cannot do

The coach answers questions about your own tracked data. It is not a doctor, a therapist or a dietitian: it gives no medical, psychological or nutritional advice and no diagnosis. If something you are dealing with needs a professional, please talk to one.

Türkçe

Giriş yapamıyorum

LiLove hesap gerektirir; alışkanlıkların ve hedeflerin sunucumuzda durur. Apple veya Google ile kaydolduysan ilk seferki düğmeyi kullan — aynı adresle bile olsa e-posta/parola hesabı ile "Apple ile Giriş" hesabı iki ayrı hesaptır.

Aboneliği yeni cihazda geri yükleme

Giriş yap, Premium ekranını aç, Restore Purchases'a dokun. Abonelik Apple hesabına bağlıdır ve her seferinde Apple'a yeniden doğrulatılır.

Jeton aldım, gelmedi

Çevrimiçiyken uygulamayı yeniden aç. Apple tamamlanmamış satın almayı yeniden sunar ve uygulama açılışta onu bitirir; jetonlar o an düşer. Aynı işlem için iki kez ücretlendirilmezsin: sunucu, daha önce gördüğü bir işlem kimliğini ikinci kez kredilendirmez.

Abonelik yönetimi ve iptal

iPhone Ayarlar → adın → Abonelikler → LiLove. Dönem bitiminden en az 24 saat önce iptal edilmezse yenilenir. İade işlemleri Apple'dadır: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Hesabı silme

Uygulamada Profil → Ayarlar → Hesabı sil. Bu, aktif bir App Store aboneliğini iptal etmez; onu iPhone Ayarlar'dan iptal etmen gerekir.

Çevrimdışı çalışır mı?

Hayır. İçeriğin sunucuda durduğu için LiLove'ın bağlantıya ihtiyacı var. Bunu uçakta öğrenmen yerine burada açıkça yazmayı tercih ediyoruz.
