To help us answer faster, please include your device model, the iOS version, and the app version (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → LiLove).

Common questions

What is included in the upcoming version? Create goals, add tasks and track daily habits. On Home, choose an active goal, see its next task, and mark it complete. The past seven days show tasks you marked complete for that goal.

Is AI coaching available? AI coaching and new subscription purchases are unavailable in this version. There is no need to retry the coach.

Do I need an account and internet? Yes, to save and retrieve your personal records. Account-linked goals, tasks and habits are stored through LiLove's server. The sample tour does not save a personal account history.

How do I cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LiLove. Cancelling there stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.

How do I check an existing subscription? If you have one, open Profile → Subscription for management or Restore Purchases. If verification is incomplete, the app shows an unverified state. If the problem continues, email us with your app version and roughly when you purchased. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

Deleting my account or data. Removing the app does not delete your server account or cancel an Apple subscription. To request account deletion or ask about the status of an existing request, email the support address above. A request is not confirmation that deletion has completed. See the privacy policy for data handling and retention.
