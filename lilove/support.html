Common questions

Does the coach read my habit names or my notes? No. What is sent is a numeric summary — streak lengths, completion rates, which hour of the day things get done. Titles, note text and task names stay on your device. The coach is told explicitly that it has not seen your words, so if it ever appears to know what a habit is about, it is guessing, and it is instructed not to.

The coach said it does not have enough data. That is deliberate. A coach that speaks confidently without evidence is worse than no coach, so when the numbers are too thin the app says so rather than inventing an insight.

The coach did not answer at all. If the model returns something that does not match the expected shape, the app reports that the coach could not answer right now instead of showing a pre-written sentence dressed up as an answer. Try again in a moment; if it keeps happening, email us.

Can I use LiLove without the coach? Yes. Habits, goals, tasks, streaks and statistics all work with no network at all. The coach is the only part that needs a connection.

How do I cancel a subscription? Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LiLove. Cancelling there stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.

I paid but Pro is not showing. Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not appear, email us with the Apple ID region you purchased in and roughly when.

What is the Team plan? A multi-seat plan for an organisation that wants LiLove for a group. It is not an individual upgrade — if you are one person, Pro is the plan you want.

Deleting my data. Your habits, goals and notes live on your device; deleting the app deletes them. Nothing about you is stored on our side to delete — see the privacy policy for exactly what is and is not transmitted.
