Habits, goals and daily tasks — with a coach that answers from your own numbers.

Track habits and their streaks, break goals into steps you can actually finish, and check off the day. The statistics show when you follow through and when you do not — by hour, by weekday, over time. Achievements mark real milestones rather than participation.

Your account carries your data

LiLove signs you in and keeps your habits, goals and notes on our server. That is a trade, and it is worth naming both sides: your content survives a lost phone and appears wherever you sign in — and it means LiLove needs a connection, and that we hold your data. What we hold and who processes it is written out in full on the privacy page, including the fact that the coach reads what you type.

The coach

Ask about your own tracked data and get an answer grounded in it. When the numbers are too thin to support a claim, it says so instead of guessing. It gives no medical, psychological or nutritional advice and makes no diagnosis.

Free, Pro and Team

Habits, goals, tasks, streaks, the day view and achievements are free, up to a limit on how many goals and habits you keep at once. Pro removes that limit, opens the full statistics view and unlocks the coach. Team is a multi-seat plan for an organisation. Coins are an optional one-time purchase spent on avatar items — you also earn them by finishing what you started.

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account and renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
