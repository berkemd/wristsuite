LiLove

Habits, goals, and a coach that never reads your words

What it does
LiLove tracks habits, goals and daily tasks on your device. Its coach looks at your progress and tells you what the numbers actually show — and it does that without ever seeing what you wrote.
The coach receives numbers, not text
Habit titles, note contents and task names never leave your device. What is sent to the coach is a numeric summary: streak lengths, completion rates, which hour of the day things get done. The coach is told, in its own instructions, that it has not seen your words and must not pretend otherwise — and that when the numbers are too thin to support a claim, it should say so instead of guessing.
