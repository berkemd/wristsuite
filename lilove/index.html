The updated iOS app is being prepared for release. The features below describe that version.
What it does
Choose an active goal on Home, see its next task, and mark that step complete. Review the tasks you marked complete over the past seven days for that goal. You can also create and track daily habits. Saving personal records requires an account and an internet connection.
Current availability
AI coaching and new subscription purchases are unavailable in this version. If you have an existing subscription, management and Restore Purchases remain accessible from Profile → Subscription. See the support page for account or subscription questions.
