Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last updated / Son güncelleme: 9 August 2026 · 9 Ağustos 2026
English first, Türkçesi aşağıda.

Forgn does not collect any data. Your archive, your notes, your projects and your plans are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere — not to us, not to anyone.

There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server operated by us.

One exception, and it is yours to trigger: when you run a literature search or clip a web page, your device contacts that public service directly. Only what you typed goes out.

1. Who we are

Forgn is developed by Berke Kahraman (Istanbul, Türkiye), the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR and the KVKK. Contact: brkekahraman@icloud.com.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create in Forgn is written to the app's private storage on your iPhone and stays there:

  • Archive entries: your notes, clipped text, scanned text, dictated text, tags.
  • Projects, plan steps, the status you mark and the notes you attach to a step.
  • Suggestions you saved, hid, or turned into a project.
  • Your app settings, including whether Face ID unlock is on.

None of this is transmitted. If you delete the app, it is deleted with it — we hold no copy and could not restore it. Use Export before deleting if you want to keep it.

3. What leaves your device, and only when you ask

Three actions reach the network. Each is started by you, each sends only what is listed, and in each case your device talks to that service directly — there is no server of ours in between, so we never see the request at all.

WhenWhat is sentTo whom
You run a prior-art / literature search The search words in the box, and your device's IP address (unavoidable in any internet request) Crossref and arXiv
You save a web page by address A normal web request for the address you entered, and your IP address The website you chose
You tap a patent database link Nothing from the app — your browser opens with the search words in the URL Google Patents, Espacenet, or Lens

Your archive is never included in any of these. The search words are not stored by us, not logged by us, and not associated with you by us, because they never reach us.

4. Camera, photos and dictation

Forgn asks for no camera permission and no photo library permission, because it needs neither. Scanning a printed page uses your iPhone's built-in text recognition from the keyboard: iOS opens the camera, recognises the text on the device, and types the result into the app. Forgn never receives an image and no photo is saved.

Voice notes use the iOS dictation key on the keyboard. Speech handling is Apple's; see Apple's privacy documentation for how dictation works on your device.

5. Face ID

If you turn on Face ID unlock, iOS performs the check and tells the app only "succeeded" or "failed". Your biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave and is never available to Forgn.

6. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. Forgn receives only a verified receipt of what was purchased and until when. We do not see, receive or store your name, email, card, or any payment detail. Manage or cancel a subscription in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

7. Children

Forgn is a tool for research and invention and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone — of any age — because we do not collect information at all.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, the KVKK and similar laws you have rights of access, correction, deletion, portability and objection. In Forgn these are unusually direct, because your data never left you:

  • Access and portability: Settings → Export produces your complete archive as a JSON file.
  • Correction: edit any entry in the app.
  • Deletion: delete an entry, or delete the app to remove everything.

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase on request. If you believe otherwise, write to us and we will investigate and answer.

9. Changes

If a future version of Forgn ever sends something new, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the App Store release notes. The date at the top always reflects the current text.

Türkçe özet

Forgn hiçbir veri toplamaz. Arşiviniz, notlarınız, projeleriniz ve planlarınız yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır ve hiçbir yere gönderilmez. Hesap yok, analitik yok, reklam yok, izleme yok ve bize ait bir sunucu yok.

Tek istisna sizin başlattığınız aramadır: literatür araması yaptığınızda yalnızca yazdığınız sözcükler, doğrudan cihazınızdan Crossref ve arXiv'e gider; bir web sayfasını kaydettiğinizde cihazınız o adresi doğrudan çeker. Arada bizim sunucumuz olmadığı için bu istekleri biz hiç görmeyiz.

Kamera ve fotoğraf izni istenmez: sayfa tarama, iOS'un klavyedeki kendi metin tanımasını kullanır ve fotoğraf uygulamaya hiç ulaşmaz. Face ID kontrolünü iOS yapar; biyometrik veriniz uygulamaya verilmez. Abonelikler Apple tarafından işlenir; ödeme bilgilerinizi görmeyiz.

KVKK ve GDPR kapsamındaki erişim, düzeltme, silme ve taşınabilirlik haklarınız uygulama içinden doğrudan kullanılabilir: Ayarlar → Dışa Aktar tüm arşivinizi JSON olarak verir; uygulamayı silmek her şeyi siler. Bizde size ait bir veri bulunmadığı için iletilecek, düzeltilecek veya silinecek bir kayıt da yoktur.
