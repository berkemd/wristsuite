Privacy Policy / Gizlilik Politikası

Last updated / Son güncelleme: August 1, 2026 · 1 Ağustos 2026
English below, Türkçesi aşağıda. Both versions say the same thing; if they ever differ, the English text is the reference. / İki sürüm aynı şeyi söyler; bir farklılık olursa İngilizce metin esas alınır.

English

GesturAir collects no data. None. When it recognises a gesture it broadcasts exactly one ASCII byte over local Bluetooth to the computer you paired it with — and never touches the internet.

Motion

Accelerometer and gyroscope readings are used only while the app is open on your wrist, and are classified on the watch. Raw motion is never stored and never sent anywhere.

What the Bluetooth link sends

When a gesture is recognised, GesturAir broadcasts exactly one ASCII byte over local Bluetooth LE — N (next), P (previous) or T (black screen). Nothing else is ever transmitted: no location, no identity, no device list, no file. The link is local radio between your watch and the computer you paired it with, it only runs while the app is in the foreground, and it never touches the internet.

The receiver page

The free receiver at berkemd.github.io/wristsuite/receiver/ is one static HTML page. It has no server, no accounts, no cookies and no storage. Your deck is read inside your own browser with the File API and is never uploaded. Its one external dependency is the pdf.js renderer (pinned version, loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com) and it is fetched only when you actually open a PDF; if that CDN is blocked, everything except PDF rendering still works. Loading a script from a CDN means that CDN sees your IP address and browser user-agent, as it would for any web page — we receive nothing from it and set no cookie of our own.

Legal basis for anything that happens

  • Whatever GesturAir keeps on your watch — we are not processing it, because it never reaches us. It sits in your device, under your control, and is gone when you delete the app. There is no legal basis to state for us, because there is no processing by us.
  • Motion permission — granted by you in the system prompt, revocable at any time in Settings; consent, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) / KVKK Art. 5(1). Motion is classified on the watch and never reaches us.
  • If you email us — we process your address and whatever you write, in order to answer you. Legal basis: GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) (steps taken at your request) and Art. 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in supporting the people who bought the app). Under KVKK, Art. 5(2)(c) and 5(2)(f).
  • Your purchase — the sale is a contract between you and Apple. Apple is the merchant of record and its own controller; we receive no name, address, email or payment detail from it, only anonymous sales totals in App Store Connect.

There is no automated decision-making and no profiling of any kind, in the sense of GDPR Art. 22 or KVKK Art. 11(g).

Your rights

Under the GDPR (Articles 15–22) and KVKK (Article 11) you have the right to ask us for: access to any personal data we hold about you; rectification of anything inaccurate; erasure; restriction of processing; portability of data you gave us, in a machine-readable form; and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where you gave consent — for example a permission prompt — you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting anything done before. KVKK Art. 11 additionally gives you the right to learn whether your data is processed, to be told the purpose, to learn the third parties it is transferred to, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to exercise them: email brkekahraman@icloud.com and say what you want. We reply within one month (GDPR Art. 12(3)); KVKK applications are answered within 30 days under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. There is no charge.

In practice, for most of it you do not need us. The data GesturAir works with lives on your watch. Delete it in the app, revoke a permission in Settings, or delete the app — and it is gone. We could not retrieve, restore or hand it over even if you asked us to, because we never had a copy.

Complaining about us

Tell us first if you can — it is usually faster. But you do not have to, and you always have the right to complain to a supervisory authority:

  • Türkiye: Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurumu / Kurulu — kvkk.gov.tr
  • EU / EEA: the data protection authority of the country where you live, work, or where you think the problem happened — the list is at edpb.europa.eu
  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office — ico.org.uk

How long anything is kept

  • On your watch: for as long as you keep it there. GesturAir does not expire or auto-delete your content; deleting the app removes its container and everything in it.
  • Support email: kept only as long as we need it to deal with your message, and deleted within 12 months at the latest — unless a law requires us to keep a specific record longer (for example Turkish tax and commercial bookkeeping rules, if your message relates to a purchase).
  • Everything else: nothing, because there is nothing. We run no database, keep no backup of your content, and write no log file that contains your data.

International transfers

  • We do not transfer your data anywhere, because we do not receive it.
  • Email is the one exception. If you write to us, your message reaches us in Türkiye — outside the EEA and the UK, and Türkiye has no EU adequacy decision. That transfer happens because you chose to contact us and is necessary to answer you (GDPR Art. 49(1)(b)). We use it for nothing else and pass it to nobody.
  • The receiver web page fetches the pdf.js renderer from cdnjs.cloudflare.com when you open a PDF. Cloudflare operates that CDN globally and will see your IP address and user-agent, as any CDN would. We receive nothing from it.

Children

GesturAir is rated 4+ and is not directed at children in particular. It collects nothing from anyone, children included, so no parental consent mechanism is needed or offered.
