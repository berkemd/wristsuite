The alert never arrived

Nestria's alert is scheduled on the family member's phone in advance, so it needs notification permission there. Open the app → Settings → Notifications. If it says Off, tap “Turn on notifications”. Without permission the app can still show today's answer when opened, but it cannot tell you on its own — and the whole point is that you stop having to check.

I tapped but they don't see it

The check-in is written to your shared iCloud area. If the phone was offline, it is kept and sent as soon as there is a connection — nothing is lost. Make sure both people are signed in to iCloud and that the family member has accepted the share invitation.

Changing when the day is expected

On the family member's phone: Settings → Expected by, and Then wait. Nestria deliberately limits these to a calm range (between 8:00 and 22:00, with at least half an hour of grace) so that the app can never turn into an alarm.

I want to switch roles

Settings → My part. Nothing already recorded is lost.

Deleting everything

Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. The shared area lives in your own iCloud; you can remove it from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
