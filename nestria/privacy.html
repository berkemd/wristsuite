Nestria collects no data.
There is no account, no password, no analytics, no advertising and no server of our own. Nothing about you is ever sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What is stored, and where

A daily check-in is a date, a timestamp, how it was sent (tap or watch) and a random device identifier. That's all. It is written to a shared area of your own iCloud account using Apple's own sharing, and it is readable only by the people you invited.

The random identifier is not a person: it carries no name, no email and no account information. It exists so that two phones in the same household can agree on which record came first.

What is never collected

No location. No microphone. No health or fitness data. No contacts. No advertising identifier. No usage analytics of any kind.

Notifications

Notification permission is used for exactly one thing: to tell the family member when a day has passed without word. The notification is scheduled locally on their device in advance and cancelled when word arrives. No push messages are ever sent from a server, because there is no server.

Children

Nestria is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
