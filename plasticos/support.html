Common questions

I paid but the content is still locked.

Open the Plans screen and tap Restore purchases. That asks Apple directly what your Apple Account owns. If it still does not unlock, make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions live in your Apple Account, not in the app, so the app cannot cancel them. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then PlasticOS. Access continues until the end of the period you have paid for.

Does my progress sync between devices?

No, and this is a deliberate limitation. PlasticOS has no server, which is why it collects nothing and works with no connection at all. The cost of that is that progress lives on one device and does not transfer.

What is Consult?

A search across the 86 procedures in the app's atlas. You ask a clinical question and it returns the passages that match, each with its primary literature citation. It works offline and it writes nothing itself — it does not compose answers, it finds them. If the atlas has no passage for your question, it says so rather than showing you a weak match.

Something in the content looks wrong.

Please tell us, and say which procedure and which step. Corrections to the surgical content are the most useful mail we get.
