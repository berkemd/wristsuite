PlasticOS

Privacy Policy

PlasticOS collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no server. Everything stays on your device.

Overview Privacy Terms Support

PlasticOS collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of ours. The app makes no network requests at all. Everything you do stays in the app's private storage on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your completed topics, quiz scores and spaced-repetition schedule are written to the app's private container on your device. That container is isolated from other apps by iOS and covered by your device passcode and device encryption. None of it is transmitted, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

What we receive

Nothing. We do not operate a server, we hold no database, and we have no way to identify you or to see how you use the app. If you email us for support, we see only what you choose to write in that email.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. The app never sees your name, card or payment details; it only asks the operating system whether a valid purchase exists. Apple's own privacy policy governs that transaction.

Children

PlasticOS is professional education material for surgical trainees and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app. That removes every piece of data it holds, permanently. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send us.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will say so before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match.
