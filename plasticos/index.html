PlasticOS

The plastic surgery residency curriculum as an atlas you can actually search — 86 procedures, 122 topics by training level, and it all works with the aeroplane mode switch on.

No account. No server. No analytics. Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign out of.

What is inside

  • 86 procedures — anatomy before you scrub, operative steps, hazards and how they are avoided, outcomes.
  • 122 curriculum topics organised by training level, PGY-1 through PGY-6.
  • Consult — a search that returns cited passages from the atlas. It retrieves; it does not generate text.
  • Case studies, decision algorithms and anatomy visualisations.
  • Everything ships inside the app and is available offline.

Plans

PGY-1 topics are free, permanently. Resident and Fellow unlock the rest; Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Details and the cancellation terms are on the subscription terms page.

Who it is for

Plastic surgery residents and fellows. PlasticOS is a study and reference tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat anyone, and it does not replace supervision, institutional protocol or clinical judgement.
