CORE PRINCIPLES · WOUND HEALING

In an experimental mouse skin-wound model, early depletion of one cell population reduces vascularised granulation tissue and impairs epithelialisation. Which cell best fits this role?

Choose one answer

Your answer stays on this page. It is not sent or saved.

Best answer: macrophages.

Macrophages help coordinate the shift from inflammation to repair. In this mouse experiment, removing them during the early phase reduced vascularised granulation tissue and impaired epithelialisation.

The useful distinction: the effect depends on timing. No statistically significant repair effect was detected after late-phase depletion in that model. This does not establish a treatment rule for a human wound.

Read the study: Lucas et al., 2010 · Review: Eming et al., 2014
