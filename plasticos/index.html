A review session, in three steps

A preview of the free spaced-repetition workflow.

  1. 01

    Try a question

    Open Spaced Repetition from Learn. Give yourself a moment to recall the answer.

  2. 02

    Read the explanation

    Reveal the answer and its explanation before choosing your rating.

  3. 03

    Rate your recall

    Tap Again, Hard, Good or Easy to record how well you recalled it and move to the next card. Your progress stays on this device.

A study companion

PlasticOS brings surgical study topics, an atlas search and review questions into one app. Use it for learning and revision alongside your training.

It is an educational reference, not a tool for diagnosing or treating patients. It does not replace supervision or your institution’s protocols.

Start with the free content

Selected PGY-1 study topics are available for free. Resident and Fellow subscriptions expand access.

Español — Estudia cirugía, una pregunta a la vez

Abre una pregunta de estudio, lee la explicación y elige cuánto recordabas. PlasticOS guarda tu progreso de repaso en el dispositivo.

Un vistazo al flujo gratuito de repetición espaciada.
