A surgical atlas and residency curriculum for plastic surgery trainees. 126 topics, 86 procedures broken down step by step, and every passage traceable to the paper it came from.

What it is

PlasticOS covers the plastic surgery residency curriculum from PGY-1 to PGY-6: the anatomy, the pre-operative assessment, the operative steps with their technical pearls and hazards, donor site management, post-operative monitoring and reported outcomes. Each of the 86 procedures carries its primary reference — Millard on cleft lip, Furlow on the double-opposing Z-plasty, Ramirez on component separation, and so on.

How it behaves

It works entirely offline. All content ships inside the app, nothing is downloaded, and the app makes no network requests. It collects no data and has no accounts. Consult, the search across the atlas, runs on the device and returns cited passages rather than generated text.

Plans

Free covers the PGY-1 fundamentals permanently. Resident and Fellow are subscriptions with a 7-day free trial from Apple, and Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Prices are shown inside the app in your own currency.
