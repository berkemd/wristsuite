To make the answer useful, please include your device model, the iOS version, the app version, and — if it is about a particular work — its DOI.

Why does a paper say “metadata only” when I can read it online?

Readable and mineable are different permissions. A paper can be free to read while its publisher has reserved text-and-data-mining rights. Stemmata reports the second, and shows the licence or opt-out clause the decision came from.

What does “the reservation set is stale” mean?

Permission decisions rest on publisher opt-out data that has a date. When that data is more than 180 days old, the card says so rather than presenting an old answer as a current one. The decision is not hidden; its age is.

Search finds nothing for a word I know is in a paper

Search runs entirely on your device and covers the metadata and any text you have imported. It cannot search a full text you do not have. For inflected languages, try a shorter stem — the trigram leg is built for exactly that case.

I edited the same note on two devices

Both edits survive. Notes merge character by character rather than last-writer-wins, so nothing is silently discarded.

A work is missing from my export

Works whose permission is closed are excluded from export by design. The export summary lists what was left out and why.

Data, deletion, refunds

Stemmata stores nothing on a server, so there is no account to close. Deleting the app removes the library. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple: report a problem.
