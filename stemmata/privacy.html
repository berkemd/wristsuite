Privacy

Stemmata — last updated 25 August 2026

The short version

There is no Stemmata server. We do not have an account system, we do not collect analytics, and we never see your library, your notes or your searches.

What stays on your device

  • Your library: works, permissions, licences and the reasons behind them.
  • Your notes and highlights.
  • Your search history and index.
  • App settings.

These are stored in the app's own container. If you use iCloud backup or iCloud Drive, they are covered by Apple's terms and encryption, and Apple — not Stemmata — holds them.

What leaves your device, and when

  • Importing a record. When you import from a public catalogue (OpenAlex, Crossref, DOI resolution), the request carries the identifier or the search terms you typed. It carries no account, no device identifier and no other part of your library. Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Opening a DOI link. The link is handed to your browser. From that point your browser, not Stemmata, is talking to the publisher.

That is the complete list. If you never import and never tap a link, the app makes no network requests at all.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email address collected.
  • No analytics, crash reporting to third parties, advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No data sold, shared or brokered — there is nothing collected to sell.

Children

Stemmata is a research tool and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected, there is no account to delete and no export to request from us. Deleting the app removes the library from the device. Under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK, Stemmata is not a data controller or processor for your library content in this mode.

Changes and contact
