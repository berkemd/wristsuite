Three things that do not change.
Safety features are not sold. Rodewatch's anchor alarm, Trailhome's way back, Nestria's check-in — the part that protects someone is free, and stays free. A paywall in front of an alarm is not a business model.
The work happens on the device. Where an app has no networking code at all, its page says so plainly. Where something does leave — one app sends numbers, never your words — the privacy page names exactly what.
No account, no sign-in, no advertising identifier. Nothing here builds a profile of you.
