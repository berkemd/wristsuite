A thought from your walk can become a note in your next project. Send a text note to Neuma, choose its topic and keep it alongside the rest of your notes.

01

Find the thought

On iPhone, open a text note in VoxWhisper and choose Send to Neuma. You can try this with a new typed note before recording anything on your watch.

02

Choose where it belongs

In Neuma, review the text, adjust its title and choose a topic such as Projects or Reading. Nothing is saved there until you choose Save.

03

Come back to it

The saved note opens in Neuma. Its VoxWhisper source and the text note’s creation date are kept. The original stays in VoxWhisper. This is a one-way text copy, not ongoing sync between the apps.

Try it without VoxWhisper Pro. Sending an accessible text note does not require Pro. Neuma is a separate app with its own optional paid features; a VoxWhisper subscription does not unlock them. View Neuma on the App Store.

What is sent, and what if the note is too long?

The handoff contains the note’s text, identifier and date, not its audio. It is designed for short notes, up to 8,000 UTF-8 bytes; the number of characters varies by language. For longer notes, use Copy or Share instead. If Neuma is not installed, the button opens its App Store card.

Before you install

Where is the iPhone app?

The iPhone companion runs on iOS 17 or later. Read, search and write text notes, or copy and share them. With Pro, voice memos recorded on Apple Watch are copied directly to iPhone for playback and sharing.

Do my recordings become text notes?

Yes, with Pro on a compatible iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Tap Transcribe for a received Watch recording and allow speech recognition. Language and model support vary by device; a model download may be needed. Search or copy the transcript, or choose Save as note to create a text note. After you enable transcription, newly received recordings can be transcribed automatically while Pro and permission remain available.

Where are my notes stored?

Text is saved locally. Eligible text syncs through your own Apple Account’s iCloud key-value store, subject to its limits. Audio originals remain on Apple Watch; the companion can receive direct copies on iPhone without sending audio through iCloud. Transcription runs on the iPhone, with no developer-operated transcription server.

Can I send a note to someone?

Yes. Open the text note and tap Send. Available destinations depend on the apps and accounts configured on your watch. On iPhone, you can also copy or share a text note.

How do I cancel Pro?

Manage the subscription through your Apple Account subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Keep the app installed if you need its locally stored notes or audio.
