What the app is. One tap opens the watch's own dictation and the note is saved the moment you stop speaking. VoxWhisper contains no speech-recognition code of its own; watchOS does the transcribing. Audio memos are recorded locally for when talking is faster than dictating.

Subscription (VoxWhisper Pro). VoxWhisper Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription that unlocks the full note history, audio memos, and complications. The free tier lists the 25 most recent notes. Nothing is ever deleted: notes beyond the 25 are kept and reappear in full if you subscribe.

  • The subscription renews automatically unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your Apple Account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Purchases and refunds. All purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own terms; we cannot issue them ourselves. Contact Apple Support for billing questions.

Privacy. VoxWhisper has no account and no ads. Text notes are written to your own iCloud key-value store, which belongs to your Apple Account; we have no server in that chain and cannot read them. Audio memos never leave the watch. See the Privacy Policy.
