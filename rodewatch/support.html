Contact

Write to brkekahraman@icloud.com. We answer every message, usually within two business days.

To help us answer faster, please include your device model, the iOS version, and the app version (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Rodewatch).

Common questions

The app is not behaving as expected. Force-quit the app and reopen it. If the problem stays, tell us what you did and what happened — a screenshot or screen recording is the fastest way for us to reproduce it.

Data and privacy. See the Privacy Policy. The short version is on that page in plain language, not only in legal terms.

Accessibility. If something is unusable with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type or Reduce Motion, that is a bug to us. Please write and we will fix it.

Reporting a problem you consider serious

If you believe the app has caused harm, lost your data, or handled your information incorrectly, say so in the subject line and we will treat it first.
