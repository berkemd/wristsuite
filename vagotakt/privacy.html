English

VagoTakt collects no data. None. It reads three numbers from your own Health app — read-only, never writing — draws them on the watch, and sends them nowhere.

Health data: read-only, three types, on the watch

VagoTakt asks your Health app for read access to exactly three things:

  • heart rate,
  • heart rate variability (HRV, SDNN),
  • resting heart rate.

It requests no write access at all — the code passes an empty "share" set — so VagoTakt cannot add, change or delete anything in your Health app. The NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription string exists only because HealthKit requires the key to be present, and it says exactly that.

What it reads: the latest sample of each, up to 28 days of HRV and resting heart rate to compute your personal medians, and up to seven daily HRV averages for the trend chart. All of it is read into memory on the watch, used to draw the screen and the balance score, and discarded when the app closes. It is not copied into the app's own storage, not exported, and not transmitted. Heart-rate data is special category data under GDPR Art. 9 and özel nitelikli kişisel veri under KVKK Art. 6 — which is precisely why the app touches it as little as possible and never moves it.

What is stored, and where

In the app's own preferences on the watch: the breathing pattern you last chose and the session length you last set. That is all. No session history, no health samples, no diary.

Purchases and the subscription

The app is a one-time purchase; VagoTakt Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription. Everything about it is handled by Apple through StoreKit: Apple is the merchant of record, takes the payment and holds the billing relationship. What the app receives is a cryptographically signed entitlement, valid for this app on this device — no name, no email address, no card details, no Apple Account identifier. We can see anonymous, aggregated sales and subscription totals in App Store Connect, and nothing that identifies you. The subscription terms are on the Subscription Terms page; the licensed agreement is the Apple Standard EULA.

Network use: none, at all

VagoTakt contains no networking code of its own — no HTTP client, no sockets, no iCloud sync, no analytics, no third-party SDK. The only traffic the app can cause is StoreKit talking to Apple to fetch prices and validate your purchase, which is Apple's own connection under Apple's own terms.

A word about what this app is

VagoTakt is a wellness app. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, prevent or monitor any disease or condition, it is not a medical device, and it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified professional.
