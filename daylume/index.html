Know when the light changes

Golden hour, blue hour, sunrise and sunset on your Apple Watch. Check the next light change, plan another date, and set a local reminder before you head out. Astronomical calculations run on the watch, even offline with a saved location.

Plan a shoot from your wrist

Choose landscape, portrait or city photography. See a suggested golden or blue hour window with its start, end and a practical shooting tip. Preview a shareable plan before opening the system share sheet. The shared text leaves out your coordinates and place name.

Start free. Add Pro when you need it.

Light times, the day planner, alerts and complications are free. Daylume Pro adds seven days of sky forecasts, the live Sun/Moon compass, and Moon and dark-sky planning. Choose a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription; the local price and period appear before purchase. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account. Photo plans are also free.

Light times and sky forecasts

Sun and Moon times are astronomical estimates shown in your watch’s time zone. Check an Apple Weather forecast for the next golden or blue hour when available; Pro adds forecasts for seven days. Forecasts need internet and cannot guarantee clear skies. Clouds, terrain and buildings can change the light you see. Near the poles, some events do not happen. The live compass needs compatible compass hardware and location permission.
