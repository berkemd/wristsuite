What is Daylume?

A light assistant that lives on your Apple Watch. Golden hour and blue hour countdowns, a live sun & moon compass, a day planner for any date, moon phases and tonight's astro-darkness window — with a gentle tap on your wrist before the light turns. Every calculation runs offline on the watch, and by default the app makes no network requests at all. No subscription, ever.

FAQ

• Does it need an iPhone? No — Daylume is a standalone watch app. • Does it work offline? Yes — every sun/moon calculation is done on the watch and your last location is remembered. By default Daylume makes no network requests at all; the one thing it can send is the optional place-name lookup to Apple's own geocoder (Settings > Location, off by default). • Which watches? Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. The compass view uses built-in compass hardware (Series 5+). • Subscription? Never. One purchase, yours forever.

Privacy: Daylume collects no data. None.
