Know when the light changes

Golden hour, blue hour, sunrise and sunset on your Apple Watch. Check the next light change, plan another date, and set a local reminder before you head out. Astronomical calculations run on the watch, even offline with a saved location.

Coming in Daylume 1.5: plan a shoot

Choose landscape, portrait or city photography. See a suggested golden or blue hour window with its start, end and a practical shooting tip. Preview a shareable plan before opening the system share sheet. The shared text leaves out your coordinates and place name. This update is awaiting release; these features are not in the current App Store version yet.

Start free. Add Pro when you need it.

Light times, the day planner, alerts and complications are free. Daylume Pro adds the live Sun/Moon compass and Moon and dark-sky planning. Choose a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription; the local price and period appear before purchase. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account. The new photo plans in 1.5 will also be free.

A plan for the light, not a weather forecast

Times are astronomical estimates shown in the watch’s time zone. Clouds, terrain and buildings can change the light you see. Near the poles, some events do not happen; Daylume shows that instead of inventing a time. The live compass needs compatible compass hardware and location permission.
