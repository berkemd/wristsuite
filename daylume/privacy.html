English

No account or tracking server. Calculations stay on your watch; Apple purchases, optional place-name lookup and sharing may use the network.

On your watch

Daylume stores your last coordinate and its time, an optional place name, alert preferences and app settings in the app’s local container, shared with its complications. Version 1.5 also stores your chosen photo goal. The developer does not receive your location or photo plans and operates no tracking or account server.

Location and optional place names

Location permission is used to calculate Sun and Moon events. The place-name lookup is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, your coordinate is sent to Apple’s geocoding service. Turning it off clears the cached name. Without a connection, the app can calculate using its saved location.

Apple services and purchases

Sun and Moon calculations do not require a server. StoreKit may contact Apple to load subscription products, process purchases, restore purchases and check entitlement. Apple handles purchase information under its own privacy terms. The app receives the transaction information needed to unlock Pro; we do not operate a payment or purchase-validation server.

Sharing in the upcoming 1.5 update

Sharing is optional. You first see the complete message: photo goal, light phase, date and time interval, watch time zone and an App Store link. Coordinates and place names are excluded. If you continue, watchOS and the app or recipient you choose handle that text under their own terms. Nothing is sent merely by viewing the preview.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (Articles 15–22) and KVKK (Article 11) you have the right to ask us for: access to any personal data we hold about you; rectification of anything inaccurate; erasure; restriction of processing; portability of data you gave us, in a machine-readable form; and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where you gave consent — for example a permission prompt — you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting anything done before. KVKK Art. 11 additionally gives you the right to learn whether your data is processed, to be told the purpose, to learn the third parties it is transferred to, and to claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

How to exercise them: email brkekahraman@icloud.com and say what you want. We reply within one month (GDPR Art. 12(3)); KVKK applications are answered within 30 days under the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller. There is no charge.

In practice, for most of it you do not need us. The data Daylume works with lives on your watch. Delete it in the app, revoke a permission in Settings, or delete the app — and it is gone. We could not retrieve, restore or hand it over even if you asked us to, because we never had a copy.

  • Everything else: nothing, because there is nothing. We run no database, keep no backup of your content, and write no log file that contains your data.
