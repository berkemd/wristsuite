Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Pathstone collects no data. None. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no third-party code of any kind. Nothing you or your child does in the app leaves the device.

What is stored, and where

Pathstone stores a short progress record on the device itself: which tasks were completed, on which day, and the age band a grown-up selected. That record is a few hundred bytes and never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes it.

What is never collected

  • No name, e-mail address, phone number or postal address.
  • No date of birth. A grown-up chooses an age band once, behind the parental gate. Pathstone never asks how old your child is.
  • No photographs, camera or microphone access. The app requests no system permissions at all.
  • No location.
  • No contacts.
  • No advertising identifier. Pathstone shows no advertising.
  • No usage analytics, crash reporting or telemetry.

No third parties

Pathstone contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, no analytics providers and no social media components. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

Children

Pathstone is designed for children and their families. Because it collects no personal information from anyone — child or adult — there is nothing to disclose, sell, share or delete on request. This is also why Pathstone does not need, and does not ask for, verifiable parental consent for data collection: there is no data collection.

Purchases and any link that would leave the app sit behind a parental gate, as required by the App Store Review Guidelines for apps made for kids.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to personal data held by us. We hold none. If you wish to remove the on-device record, delete the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. A change that introduced any data collection would also require a new app version and a new App Store review.
