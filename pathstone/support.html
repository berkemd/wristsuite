Support

Questions, problems and how the app decides things.

How does Pathstone choose today’s task?

It follows nine capabilities and picks the one practised least, then chooses a task at the right difficulty for how the last few went. The parent dashboard shows the actual numbers used — not a summary of them.

My child said a task was hard. Did they fail?

No. “It was hard” is a signal, not a failure. The app lowers the difficulty of that capability immediately. Without that button a child would either say the task went fine when it did not, or stop using the app.

Why is there no streak counter?

Because a streak moves the reason to keep going away from the work and onto the fear of losing something. There is no defensible reason to tell a seven-year-old who missed one day that they ruined everything. Pathstone counts stones instead, and a stone once placed is never taken away.

Why are there no notifications?

Notifications exist to bring people back to a screen. This app’s promise is the opposite: a few minutes, then out of the way.

The same task came back. Is that a bug?

A task is not repeated within 21 days. After that it can return, which is deliberate — a thing practised once is not a thing learned.

Can I change the age band?

Yes, from the grown-ups screen, behind the parental gate.

I forgot the parental gate answer

The gate asks an arithmetic question written out in words, and it changes each time. Three wrong answers pause it for one minute. There is nothing to remember and nothing to reset.

Does Pathstone work offline?

Entirely. It has no server to reach.
